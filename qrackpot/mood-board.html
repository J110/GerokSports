§05 · VoiceWRITE THIS WAY

How the site actually talks.

Six sample blocks. Real copy, not lorem. This is what gets written for the v0 site if you sign off on the voice.

Hero — option 1 of 3
Sports broadcasting has been getting worse for fifty years. We fixed it.
Real-time AI commentary for every match, every language, every level. Even the ones nobody covers. Especially those.
Hero — option 2 of 3
Your nephew's U-13 cricket match deserves better than silence.
Premier League gets six commentators. Your nephew gets a guy named Vikram filming in vertical mode with no audio. We're fixing the second one.
Hero — option 3 of 3
We taught a machine to call a googly. Now we're teaching it everything else.
Cricket. Football. Kabaddi. Padel. Esports. Whatever your sport is, in whatever language you actually speak. Real-time AI commentary, live now.
The problem section
Three things wrong with watching sports in 2026.
One: nobody's actually talking to you — broadcasters talk at a million people at once.
Two: nobody's talking about you — the things you want to know, the matchups, the patterns, nobody's computing those in real time.
Three: most of the games on Earth right now? Nobody's talking at all.

We thought that was weird too.
The partner section
If you've ever looked at a silent sports feed and thought "somebody should be calling this" —
We're somebody. Three ways to work with us — pick the one that fits. Streaming platform? We have an API. Broadcaster or league? We have enterprise. Regional network with a long tail of silent matches? We have a revenue share. All three start with an email.
The close
Stop watching sports. Start participating in them again.
Get on the waitlist, or come build with us. The next match starts in about four hours and right now nobody's calling it. Let's change that.
§06 · Narrative arcSEVEN BLOCKS

The page, blocked out.

Seven blocks. Each does one thing. Total copy budget: under 800 words. If a section needs more than that, it's the wrong section.

01

The punch

Hero
Who we are in one sentence. Two CTAs.
Treatment: huge type, no hero image, tiny animated motif (waveform or scoreboard tick). 40 words max.
02

The fight

Problem
What's wrong with watching sports in 2026.
Treatment: three magazine-style statements. Big numbers, no bullets. Stamps + tape over key words.
03

The bet

Vision
What we're building, and the world it makes.
Treatment: editorial paragraph. One pull-quote in italic serif. No diagram here.
04

The long tail

Why it matters
Every sport on Earth. Every language. Even Vikram's match.
Treatment: marquee of sports + languages scrolling past, or a wall of names. Visual element, not a list.
05

The engine

Tech, briefly
Vision → Reasoning → Voice, in one sentence.
Treatment: three giant serif words on a line. No table, no vendor names. One stat: "under four seconds, frame to phrase."
06

Three doors

How to work with us
API / Enterprise / White-label · plus fan waitlist.
Treatment: four numbered cards, magazine-style, one-line value prop each. CTAs are real email links, not forms.
07

Who's behind it

Founder
One line. Patents. Prior exit. Done.
Treatment: typewritten byline + a small portrait or initials stamp. No bio card.
